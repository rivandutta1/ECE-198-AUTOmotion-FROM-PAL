## Testing Procedure

2. Ensure the hip, shoulder, elbow, and wrist are visible.
3. Keep the camera stationary throughout testing.
4. Press C with the arm down and torso upright.
5. Remain still until calibration succeeds.
6. Raise the arm overhead while remaining upright.
7. Introduce a comfortable, visible torso lean without weights.
8. Return to upright posture.
9. Hide the wrist or move partly out of frame.
10. Lower the arm and verify the inactive state.
